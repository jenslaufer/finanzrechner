Note: Calculation based on §§43, 59, 77 and 96a SGB VI. The Erwerbsminderungsrente replaces lost earnings when medical assessment confirms reduced earning capacity. Full disability pension applies when remaining capacity falls below 3 hours per day; partial pension covers 3–6 hours. The deduction is 0.3 % per month before the reference age, capped at 10.8 % (36 months). The Zurechnungszeit credits years from the disability entry to the reference age with the personal average Entgeltpunkte. 2026 earnings limits: approx. €19,661 (full) and €37,118 (partial — individual high-3 calculation).
