Household-related service (§ 35a EStG)

Tax note: Job-related moves (new job, transfer or at least one hour of commute time saved per day) are fully deductible as income-related expenses — at minimum the statutory moving allowance under § 10 BUKG (2026: €964). Private moves can only deduct the labour share of a mover bill as a household-related service (§ 35a EStG, 20 % tax credit, max €4,000/year). This calculation is an estimate — the actual deduction depends on your tax assessment.
